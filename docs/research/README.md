# Research Documentation

This directory contains methodology documents for reproducible research around AIDatasetKit. It is separate from beginner guides and does not introduce new runtime features.

| Document | Role |
|---|---|
| [AIDatasetKit Real-World Comparative Research Protocol](AIDatasetKit_Research_Protocol.md) | Research questions, public data sources, fairness criteria, stress-test design, candidate strengths/costs, threats to validity, and paper bridge. |

The protocol’s executable companion is the [Real-World Comparative Study](../../examples/research/AIDatasetKit_Real_World_Comparative_Study.ipynb). Begin with [Getting Started](../getting-started.md) and the task guides before interpreting its results. The study measures trade-offs and explicitly permits three outcomes: an observed workflow benefit, a conventional-workflow advantage, or insufficient evidence.
